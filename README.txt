// Command Line Tetris

Command to play: python tetrisPlay.py

The Player's score is calculated using two factors.

1. The amount of seconds elapsed during gameplay: The player gets 1 additional score for each 10 seconds that passes
2. The amount of score gained for completing a row: Calculated by the amount of columns the player selects at the start of the game.

These two elements are added together for the player's final score.
